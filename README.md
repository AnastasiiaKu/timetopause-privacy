# Time to Pause

## Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 1 June 2025 · **Last updated:** 25 November 2026  
**App:** Time to Pause: Symptom Tracker · **Developer:** APK Studio

---

### Overview

Time to Pause is a private symptom tracker with a guided breathing exercise. There are no accounts, no sign-ins and no ads. Your symptom diary and your preferences are stored only on your device. The app sends basic usage statistics, described below.

---

### Data Stored Only on Your Device

- Your symptom diary: daily severity scores, any symptoms or categories you add, hide or archive, and events you add to your trends (a name and a date, such as starting a medication)
- Your breathing pattern and number of cycles
- Display preferences, such as appearance (system, light or dark), the order of the Track sections and which ones are open

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us, to any server, cloud service or third party, and it is not included in the usage statistics.

**Exported reports and data.** If you choose Export trends on the Track tab, the app makes a PDF of the charts (with any events), or a CSV table of your scores, for the dates and symptoms you select, on your device. A temporary copy is kept in the app's private cache and replaced by your next export. The file only leaves your device when you choose where to send or save it in Android's share sheet; from then on it is handled by the app or service you picked.

**Uploaded data.** If you choose Upload previous data, the app reads the CSV file you pick on your device and saves its dates and scores in your symptom diary, along with any new symptoms you choose to add from it. The file itself is not kept or sent anywhere.

---

### Usage Statistics

To understand which parts of the app are used, Time to Pause uses Google Firebase Analytics. It records events such as opening the app, opening the Love tab, and starting or finishing a breathing exercise (with the pattern and number of cycles). Firebase also records standard app and device information, such as app version, device model, operating system, approximate region and a randomly generated identifier for your installation of the app. This identifier is not linked to your name or any account.

These statistics do not include your name, email address, contact details, symptom diary or events. They are processed by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

### Tips

Optional tips are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a tip was made, but never your payment details.

---

### Data Retention and Deletion

Data on your device stays there for as long as the app is installed. **When you uninstall the app, Android permanently deletes it.** You can also clear it at any time in Settings > Apps > Time to Pause > Storage > Clear data. Removing a symptom or category you added (in Customise symptoms) deletes it and its past scores.

Usage statistics are kept by Google Firebase Analytics for the retention period set for our Analytics account, and then deleted automatically.

---

### Permissions

- **INTERNET** — to send usage statistics and process tips
- **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE** — to check for a connection before sending statistics or starting a tip
- **VIBRATE** — for gentle haptic cues during breathing
- **BILLING** — for optional tips through Google Play

The app does not send notifications.

### Health Information

Time to Pause is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. The symptom diary and any report you export are for your own notes; they are not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional advice. Always consult a healthcare professional about your health. Your symptom diary is never sent to us or to the usage statistics; it only leaves your device if you choose to export and share it.

### Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

### Security

Data on your device is protected by Android's standard app sandboxing. Usage statistics are sent to Google over encrypted connections.

# Time to Pause

## Privacy Policy

Time to Pause does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDKs.

### What stays on your device

- Your bell schedule and preferences
- Pending Android alarm entries (used to ring the bell)

None of this leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it all.

### Notifications & vibration

Bells are delivered through Android's local notification and vibration systems. They do not contact any server.
